# Image briefs — Teas & Decoctions (10 entries)

Companion to `scripts/seed-pranajiva-teas.mjs`. The ten remedies are seeded without images; this
holds the prompts and the alt text so neither has to be reinvented at upload time.

The alt text matters more than the prompt. Every `coverImage` and every step image has a **required**
alt field in the schema, so a remedy cannot be published with an image and no alt text — these are
written to drop straight in.

## House style, all images

Airy ivory and cream grounds, natural tulsi and sage greens, restrained brass or gold accents.
Photorealistic Indian kitchen and ingredients, daylight, high-key. No text, no watermark, no hands.

- Cover image: **16:9**
- Step and ingredient images: **1:1**

Only give a step an image where seeing it helps — a colour to simmer to, a texture to look for. The
schema's own note is the rule: a photo of a cup does not earn its place. The published Tulsi kadha
has images on three of its five steps and none on the last two, which is about the right ratio.

## 01 · Cumin, Coriander & Fennel Tea

**Cover prompt** — Two small transparent cups of pale-golden CCF tea alongside cumin, coriander and
fennel seeds in three tiny brass bowls; high-key ivory styling, daylight, 16:9.

**Cover alt** — `Two cups of cumin, coriander and fennel tea with the whole seeds beside them`

**Steps** — close-up of the three mixed whole seeds, lightly cracked · seeds simmering in a small pan
· tea strained into two transparent cups.

## 02 · Ajwain & Saunf Kadha

**Cover prompt** — Golden transparent cup of ajwain-saunf kadha, carom and fennel seeds in separate
gold-rimmed dishes, soft ivory tabletop, botanical green accents, 16:9.

**Cover alt** — `Ajwain and saunf kadha served warm, with carom and fennel seeds alongside`

**Steps** — measured ajwain and fennel, showing how little ajwain is used · seeds at a gentle simmer ·
the finished strained infusion.

## 03 · Lemongrass & Ginger Tea

**Cover alt** — `Lemongrass and ginger tea with a fresh bruised stalk and sliced ginger`

**Steps** — a bruised lemongrass stalk beside thin ginger slices, the bruising clearly visible ·

## 06 · Dalchini & Elaichi Tea

**Cover prompt** — Warm golden cinnamon-cardamom tea, a slender cinnamon stick and green cardamom
pods beside a ceramic cup, bright ivory tabletop, muted gold detailing, 16:9.

**Cover alt** — `Dalchini and elaichi tea with a cinnamon stick and green cardamom pods`

**Steps** — one cinnamon stick and two cracked cardamom pods. **If one step image is worth shooting
properly in this whole set, it is this one: Ceylon and cassia cinnamon side by side, pale and
many-layered against dark and thick.** The body text turns on that difference and a photograph
settles it faster than a paragraph · gentle spice simmer · tea in a cup.

## Uploading

Nothing in this batch has an asset reference, real or invented. Add images in the Studio alongside
each entry and paste the alt text above into the required field — that keeps the asset ids where
Sanity manages them rather than hardcoded in a script.
